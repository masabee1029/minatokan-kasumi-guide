How to book Minatokan

An English map of a Japanese reservation page.

Your booking in 5 steps

The reservation pages are in Japanese. Your browser's translate button helps a lot, and this page tells you what each part means.

  1. Open the plan list. Open Minatokan's booking site
  2. Enter your stay. Set the date, nights, rooms and guests, then press 再検索 (search again).
  3. Compare plans. Each plan shows room types with a price per adult, tax included. Rooms marked ※バスなしトイレなし have no bath or toilet.
  4. Open your plan. Press 詳細・予約 (details and booking) and read the cancellation rules.
  5. Finish your reservation. Press 予約へ進む (go to booking), then follow the form. The comments box is where your pickup time and allergies go.

Prefer to ask first?

Email info@minatokan.jp or call +81-796-36-3330. A short message with clear dates is easy for a small ryokan to answer, and you are welcome to use this one.

Hello. I would like to stay at Minatokan.
Date: (check-in date) for (number) night(s)
Guests: (number) adults, (number) children
Room: sea-view bath room / sea-view room / mountain-side room
Arrival: JR Kasumi Station at (time). May I use the free pickup?
Question: (for example, tattoo policy, food allergy, stairs)
Thank you very much.

Telling the ryokan you are writing in English is helpful. We could not confirm how much English the staff use, so short sentences may work best.
