Around Kasumi

Sights within an hour, with driving times from the ryokan.

Pair it with Kinosaki Onsen

Kinosaki's famous bathhouse town is about 30 minutes away by limited express, so you can spend a night in each. You could enjoy Kinosaki's lanes first, then continue to Kasumi for the sunset and a quieter evening.

Amarube Bridge

Amarube Station is about 10 minutes west of Kasumi on the San'in Line local train, since the Hamakaze does not stop there. The old steel trestle was replaced by a concrete bridge in 2010, and part of it now forms the Sora no Eki observation deck, about 40 meters above the ground.

San'in Kaigan Geopark

Minatokan stands inside the San'in Kaigan National Park, on a coast recognized as a Geopark. You can read the rock and sea stacks from the sightseeing boat or from the shore.

Summer at the ryokan

In July and August, staying guests have been offered a free marine activity such as rock fishing, plus paid trial diving from ¥12,000 and a charcoal BBQ lunch by the beach. Offers change, so the ryokan can confirm this year's list.

Build your Kasumi night into the trip

You can book on Jalan, Japan's big travel site, or directly on Minatokan's own reservation site. Both are in Japanese, and this walkthrough explains each screen in English.
