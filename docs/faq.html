Frequently asked questions

The ryokan's common questions, in plain English.

Answers are rewritten in our own words from the Q&A that Minatokan publishes on Jalan and on its own website, checked November 8, 2026. Some source answers are several years old, so the ryokan's word is always the final one.

Booking and payment

Where do I book?

All reservations on this guide go to Minatokan's own booking site. The site is in Japanese, and our How to Book page translates each step.

How late can I book?

The plan we checked accepted reservations until 17:00 on the day of the stay. Limits can differ by plan.

What if my date is full?

The ryokan accepts waiting-list requests by phone or email.

Which payment methods work?

Cash or card. Jalan lists JCB, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Diners and debit cards at the ryokan, and its FAQ also mentions PayPay. An older English page says only Visa and Mastercard, so carrying some cash is a sensible backup. Online card payment at booking is offered on some plans.

When do cancellation fees begin?

Generally 30% from 7 days before, 50% from 5 days before, 70% from 3 days before and 100% on the day or for a no-show. Some plans differ, so it helps to read the plan you pick.

Do weather cancellations cost anything?

The same fees apply. December to March brings heavy snow here, so the ryokan suggests watching the forecast and changing your booking early if needed.

Can I stay alone?

The Jalan FAQ says there are no one-person plans available online, and invites solo travelers to phone the ryokan directly.

Are there extra charges?

A bath tax of ¥150 per adult is charged separately.

Rooms and stay

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 10:00. If you will arrive after 18:00, the ryokan asks you to let them know.

Do all rooms have a bath and toilet?

No. The 3 sea-view bath rooms do. The sea-side and mountain-side rooms are marked as having no bath or toilet.

Are there Western-style beds?

All 10 rooms are Japanese-style tatami rooms.

Is sitting on the floor required?

In-room meals use tables about 35 cm high. Low chairs are available for small children and anyone with difficulty sitting.

Can you hold my luggage?

Yes. The front desk can keep bags before check-in and after check-out.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, Wi-Fi is available throughout the building.

Are pets allowed?

No. The building is wooden and small, so pets cannot stay.

Is there a yukata for me?

Yes. Each room has yukata, and women can pick one more from 30 colorful patterns for free. Staff can help you dress.

Baths

When are the communal baths open?

The Japanese FAQ (2021) says 24 hours. An older English page lists 15:00 to 24:00 and 6:00 to 9:30. The front desk can confirm.

Is the private sea-view bath available all night?

Yes. It is yours at any time from check-in until check-out, and it has a shower and washing area.

Is there a communal open-air bath?

Jalan lists one indoor bath each for men and women, with no communal open-air bath or sauna.

Are tattoos allowed?

We could not find a published policy. The ryokan can give you the current answer by email or phone.

Meals and celebrations

What time is dinner?

Dinner begins from 18:00, and the ryokan asks what time you prefer.

Can I come only for a meal?

Yes, there are day-use meal plans in a private dining room. Dinner runs from 18:00 to 21:00. Bathing can be added for ¥650, which covers a rental hand towel and the bath tax.

Can you prepare a cake or champagne?

Yes, with advance notice. Birthday cakes start from ¥3,460 for a 12 cm size, and champagne and flowers can be arranged to your budget.

I have a food allergy.

The ryokan asks you to tell them beforehand, and the comments box on the booking form is a good place to do so.

Getting there

Is there a station shuttle?

Yes. The ryokan picks you up at JR Kasumi Station for free and takes you back the next day.

Is parking free?

Yes. There is room for 17 cars, and a second lot for large or low vehicles.

Is there a convenience store nearby?

Two Lawson stores are about 7 minutes away by car.

What if snow stops my trip?

Call the ryokan right away. It offers free pickup for stranded guests, including from Wadayama or Toyooka.

Children

Is there a children's breakfast?

Yes. Children can have either the adult breakfast or a children's breakfast, chosen when you book.

Are there children's yukata?

Yes, in blue and pink. Yukata for very small children may be long, so ask ahead. Rentals are ¥200 when not included.

Can you warm baby food?

Please bring your own milk or baby food. The kitchen can warm food and sterilize bottles if you ask the staff.

Summer activities

Is there a shuttle to the beach?

Yes, free shuttles run to beaches in town. Parking at Imagoura Beach by your own car costs extra.

Can I try scuba diving?

Trial dives start from ¥12,000 per person with gear and a guide, in July and August. A license course is also offered.

Can I rinse off after the beach?

Yes, there is a shower space, and guests can use a washing machine. Please check at the front desk first.
