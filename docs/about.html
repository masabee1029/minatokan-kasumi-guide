About this guide

Independent, and clear about its ads and its sources.

Thank you for being here

We appreciate your interest in Kasumi. This guide exists so that travelers who read English can discover a small ryokan on the Sea of Japan and feel confident booking it.

This is an unofficial guide

This site is independent. It is not run by, endorsed by or affiliated with Minatokan or Jalan. Reservations are made on Jalan or on the ryokan's own site, and questions about your stay go directly to the ryokan.

Affiliate ads

Links labeled "See rooms & rates" and other links to Jalan are affiliate ads through A8.net. This site earns a commission when a stay booked through one of those links is completed. Links labeled "Book direct" go to the ryokan's own site and earn this site nothing.

How it is made

  • Text was drafted with AI assistance, using the public sources below.
  • Facts are rewritten in our own words. Guest reviews and the ryokan's photos are not copied here.
  • Nothing on this site describes a personal stay.
  • Every image was created with generative AI (Google Gemini) from written descriptions, and each one is labeled. They suggest the mood only, and do not show the real ryokan, rooms, food or view. Real photos are on Jalan.
  • Sunset times are calculated estimates.

The Jalan rating and all prices were read on November 8, 2026 and can change.
