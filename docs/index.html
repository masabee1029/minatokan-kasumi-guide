10-room onsen ryokan · Kasumi, Hyogo

Your sunset over the Sea of Japan, then a table full of crab

Minatokan (三七十館) sits on the Kasumi coast, about 30 minutes by limited express beyond Kinosaki Onsen. You get an onsen bath facing the sea, seafood from the local port and a small house with only 10 rooms.

Three reasons travelers go the extra 30 minutes

A title earned by sunsets

The ryokan carries the name "Japan's 100 Most Beautiful Sunsets" in its title, and its baths look out over the sea.

Sunset times by month

Crab for most of the year

Kasumi's red snow crab season opens in September, and prized Matsuba crab follows on November 6.

Which crab, which month

Run by seafood people

Minatokan grew out of Minatozushi, a local sushi bar. Its Jalan page cites a 2025 award: first in sales among inns of 10 rooms or fewer in Kinki-Hokuriku.

Rooms and baths

Worth knowing before you choose

  • Standard rooms have no private bath or toilet. If an en-suite matters to you, the 3 sea-view bath rooms are the ones to look for.
  • All rooms are tatami, with low tables at about 35 cm. Low chairs are available if you ask.
  • The communal baths are indoors, with sea views. Private open-air bathing comes with the 3 special rooms.
  • December to March brings heavy snow here, and the usual cancellation fees still apply in bad weather.
  • Most plans are priced per adult, so a total for two is double the number you first see.
