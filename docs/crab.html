Crab, month by month

Which crab you can taste in Kasumi, and what a full-course plan costs.

Matsuba crab or Kasumi crab?

Kasumi is a real crab town, with two different stars. Your travel month decides which one you can taste.

Matsuba crabKasumi crab (Kasumi-gani)

How Minatokan serves it

Full-course dinners

Courses bring crab in several styles, such as sashimi, charcoal-grilled, shabu-shabu and boiled. A tank of live crab greets you near the entrance.

Other tastes from the area

Plans can add Tajima beef steak, live abalone, blackthroat seaperch or morning-caught squid. Choices differ by plan and season.

Snow crab, Matsuba and king crab

"Snow crab" is the species, and "Matsuba" is what people on this coast call the males. King crab is a different animal, more closely tied to northern waters such as Hokkaido, so you would not expect it as the local catch here.

Booking for crab season

With only 10 rooms, popular winter dates can fill early. When your date is full, the ryokan accepts waiting-list requests by phone or email.
