A sunset worth planning around

Month-by-month times for Kasumi, one of the coasts behind Minatokan's sunset title.

Where the name comes from

The ryokan's full name in Japanese means "the onsen inn of Japan's 100 sunset spots," and its English site calls the area "Japan's 100 Most Beautiful Sunset." Local tourism listings connect the title to this stretch of coast, naming Imagoura and Okami Park among the viewpoints.

Three places to watch

From your own bath

The 3 sea-view bath rooms have a semi-open-air tub facing the Sea of Japan, ready for you at any hour of your stay.

From the communal bath

The men's and women's baths overlook the sea. A hedge outside protects privacy, and the ryokan says the sunset still shows above it.

From Imagoura

Imagoura Beach is about 3 minutes by car, and Okami Park is a known hilltop viewpoint nearby. The front desk can point you the right way.

Your best odds

The ryokan singles out spring through summer for red sunsets and the lights of fishing boats on the horizon. Winter skies here are often unsettled, so a winter trip is usually more about crab and snow.
